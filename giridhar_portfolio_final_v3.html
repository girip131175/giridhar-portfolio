about
The short version

I'm a cybersecurity researcher and B.Tech CSE student specializing in Cybersecurity at SASTRA Deemed University. My work sits at the intersection of offensive security, AI/ML security, defensive security, and the systems required to study intelligent systems rigorously.

My research experience spans adversarial malware mutation, insider-threat detection, IIoT security, resilient AI systems, and LLM security. I am particularly interested in understanding how intelligent systems fail under adversarial conditions and then using those findings to build stronger, testable defenses.

In 2026, I worked as a Research Intern in LLM Security at Colorado State University under Prof. Indrakshi Ray. I worked on secure RAG systems, access-control and information-flow questions, NeMo Guardrails, and adversarial evaluation of LLM-based systems.
